# Floristería Order Management System

## Context

The floristry business experiences high demand during certain dates, such as Día de los Santos, when hundreds of flower orders are received daily (300 orders per Día de los Santos). Currently, all orders are managed manually on paper, creating several challenges:

- Duplicate or lost orders
- Difficulty tracking order status
- Time-consuming record keeping

## Problem Statement

The current paper-based order management process is inefficient, error-prone, and not scalable. Employees spend significant time tracking, copying, and managing orders, which leads to delays, mistakes, and occasional customer dissatisfaction.

There is no digital system to:

- Centralize orders
- Track order status
- Generate customer receipts
- Provide a clear overview for employees

## Objectives

The goal of this project is to create a digital system to manage flower orders efficiently, with the following objectives:

1. Enable employees to create, update, and track orders digitally.
2. Allow customers to receive order confirmations or receipts.
3. Provide a clear, up-to-date overview of order statuses for staff.
4. Support scalability for peak demand periods.
5. Serve as a clean, maintainable, and professional codebase for future enhancements.

## Out of Scope

- Online payment processing
- Notificaitons or alerts
- Multi-location inventory management
- Mobile app interface

These items may be considered for future development but are not part of the initial MVP.

## Current Paper Process

Customer Order -> Paper Form -> Employee Logs -> Copies for Customer & Shop -> Tracking on Paper
